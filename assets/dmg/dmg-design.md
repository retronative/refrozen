# reFrozen DMG window: design spec

This is the approved design for the DMG window background. No packaging script uses it yet: wiring
it into a DMG build (for example with `create-dmg` or an AppleScript layout step) is a separate
change. Only the source is committed. A packaging step renders the images.

## Files

| File | Committed | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `dmg-background.html` | yes | The single source of the background |
| `render.mjs` | yes | Renders the source and checks the label contrast (below) |
| `../../.website/public/banners/icegate-1920.webp` | yes (website) | The painting the source uses |
| `../../.website/public/fonts/` (Cinzel, Cormorant Garamond) | yes (website) | Fonts, SIL OFL 1.1 |
| `dmg-background.png`, `dmg-background@2x.png`, `dmg-background.tiff` | **no** | Generated by `render.mjs` into `build/dmg/` (git-ignored) |

To render (needs Google Chrome, Node 22 or later, and macOS `sips`/`tiffutil`; no npm packages),
from the repository root:

```sh
node assets/dmg/render.mjs                 # build/dmg/dmg-background.png, @2x.png, .tiff
node assets/dmg/render.mjs --out DIR       # somewhere else
node assets/dmg/render.mjs --mock          # review mock-ups with icons and labels (Dark and Light)
```

| Output | Size | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| `dmg-background.png` | 660 × 400 px | 72 dpi |
| `dmg-background@2x.png` | 1320 × 800 px | 144 dpi |
| `dmg-background.tiff` | both of the above (`tiffutil -cathidpicheck`) | 72 and 144 dpi |

Give Finder the TIFF so Retina screens get the 2x image. `render.mjs` drives headless Chrome over
the DevTools protocol at an exact 660 × 400 viewport. Chrome's own `--screenshot` flag cannot do
this: in new headless mode its `--window-size` includes the window frame, so the image comes out
wrong. Set `CHROME` if Chrome is not in `/Applications`.

`render.mjs` exits with status 1 if a label plate falls below 4.5:1 for white or black text.
`tests/test_brand_assets.py` runs it and checks the sizes and resolutions, and skips that check
with a message when Chrome or Node is missing.

## Contrast behind the icons and labels

Finder draws icon labels itself: white text in Dark appearance, black text in Light appearance.
No single dark or light background serves both. Each label therefore sits on a flat frosted-steel
plate `#697789` (relative luminance 0.180), with a gold-deep hairline. That luminance is the point
where white and black text both reach AA. The plate is flat on purpose, because any gradient or
texture would push one of the two below 4.5:1.

Measured by `render.mjs` on the 1x render (plates inset 3 px; icon wells are the 128 pt icon area
plus an 8 pt ring):

| Area | Luminance | White text | Black text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Label plate, `reFrozen.app` | 0.1800–0.1800 | 4.56:1 | 4.60:1 |
| Label plate, `Applications` | 0.1800–0.1800 | 4.56:1 | 4.60:1 |

| Icon well | Mean luminance (max) | Gold frame `#ab7f52` against the mean |
|---|---|---|
| `reFrozen.app` | 0.0391 (0.2704) | 3.31:1 |
| `Applications` | 0.0300 (0.2517) | 3.68:1 |

The icon wells are dark (the scrim darkens the painting there), so the icon's gilded frame and the
blue Applications folder both stand clear of the art at 3:1 or more.

## Visual language

- **Background**: the website's approved "icegate" banner painting (a frozen gothic gate between
  pillars, candlelight at the edges). It is scaled to 115% and moved up so the figure in the gate
  sits below the drag cue. It is darkened at the top for the wordmark and at the bottom, with warm
  candlelight at the sides and a cold blue glow in the gate: the icon's ice against antique gold.
- **Frame**: a hairline gilded border (gold-deep `#ab7f52`) 10 pt inside the window edge, with the
  website's curved-spike corners and the icon's four-point studs at the mid-edges.
- **Wordmark**: "re" in italic Cormorant with the frost gradient, "Frozen" in Cinzel with the
  antique-gold metal gradient. Same fonts and casing as the site header.
- **Tagline**: "Native on Apple Silicon" in letterspaced Cinzel, bone `#ece6d8`, above a gold rule
  with a four-point stud and an ice core.
- **Drag cue**: an ice shaft (`#4fb2eb → #88d3f9 → #dfedfa`) with a soft glow, a stud at the
  midpoint and a barbed head.
- **Alternative** (`--art vector`): the same layout in gradients and SVG only. It is not used.

## How this follows the reference mock-up and where it differs

The reference was the supplied Finder window mock-up `gmg-ref.png` (1536 × 1024 px).

Follows:
- Central frozen gate, gothic pillars and warm light at the left and right edges.
- Metallic wordmark at the top centre with an ice-blue accent, a letterspaced tagline and a gold
  rule with a diamond ornament.
- App icon left and Applications right on one line at about 60% height, labels below.
- A glowing ice arrow with a diamond at its midpoint.

Differs:
- The window is 660 × 400 (1.65:1), the standard DMG size, rather than 3:2.
- No statues, banners or snow-capped citadel: they are not in any approved artwork. The approved
  icegate painting supplies the gate, pillars and candlelight.
- The tagline is "Native on Apple Silicon" (approved).
- The labels sit on steel plates where the reference has dark pills. Dark pills fail with Finder's
  black labels in Light appearance.
- There is a gilded inner frame with studs.
